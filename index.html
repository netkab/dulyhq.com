Stablecoin infrastructure

The industry adopted stablecoins for settlement but kept the dispute, compliance, and trust processes designed for fiat. The unique properties of programmable money — conditionality, verifiability, finality — are going entirely unused.

We're building the infrastructure that actually uses them.

01

Conditionality

Payment logic encoded natively — funds move when and only when conditions are met, without manual intervention.

02

Verifiability

On-chain auditability creates an immutable record of counterparty agreements, conditions, and outcomes.

03

Finality

Atomic settlement eliminates the reconciliation overhead and counterparty exposure that fiat rails require.
